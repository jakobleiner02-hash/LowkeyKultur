Kultur Day

Kultur Day is a special event where everyone can share their traditions. Countries are invited to send videos showing their art, music, and customs. All clips are combined into one big film shown in cinemas for everyone to experience.

After the screening, the audience can vote for the most inspiring country. The winning country is invited to Haderslev to present their culture live.

The winner receives free travel and accommodation — and together we celebrate with a colorful cultural festival.
